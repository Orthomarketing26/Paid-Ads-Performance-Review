What the Ads team is doing to become more efficient

Four changes, all aimed at catching problems early and giving managers time to optimise rather than just maintain.

  • Ad Manager guidelines

    A written standard for account setup, conversion tracking, naming and budget changes, so the mistakes and loopholes found in this review are covered and cannot recur.

  • Rolling checks

    Scheduled audits on every account so problems like a zero-conversion campaign or broken tracking are caught in days, not months.

  • Monday boards

    Every account, task and owner visible in one place. Actions agreed on a call are tracked to completion, and workload across managers is transparent.

  • New team member

    Added to spread the workload, so managers have time to optimise accounts rather than just maintain them.
